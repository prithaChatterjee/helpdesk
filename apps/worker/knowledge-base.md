## Plans and pricing

- **Basic:** $10 per user per month. Up to 5 users, 10 GB storage, email support.
- **Pro:** $25 per user per month. Unlimited users, 100 GB storage, priority support (first reply
  within 4 business hours), API access and single sign-on (SSO).
- **Yearly billing:** pay yearly and get 2 months free (you pay for 10 months instead of 12).
- **Free trial:** every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of Pro. No card is needed. Cancel
  during the trial and you are never charged.
- **Payment methods:** Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Teams on yearly Pro plans with
  20 or more users can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice.

## Changing plans

- Upgrade or downgrade any time in **Settings > Billing > Change plan**.
- Upgrades apply immediately. You pay only the difference for the rest of the current billing
  period (prorated).
- Downgrades apply at the start of the next billing period. Nothing is refunded for the current
  period.

## Discounts

- **Students:** 50% off Pro with a valid university email address. Apply in **Settings > Billing >
  Discounts**; the discount is checked automatically within one business day.
- **Nonprofits:** registered charities get 30% off any plan. Email nonprofit@helpdesk.example with
  proof of registration.
- There are no other discounts.

## Invoices and receipts

- All invoices since the workspace was created are in **Settings > Billing > Invoices**, where each
  can be downloaded as a PDF.
- To put your company name, address or VAT number on future invoices, add them in **Settings >
  Billing > Billing details**. Invoices that were already issued can't be changed.

## Refunds (policy only)

- Charges can be refunded within 14 days. Duplicate charges are always refunded in full.
- **Refunds are processed by our billing team, not automatically.** A ticket that asks for a refund
  or for a charge to be reversed always needs an Agent.
- Approved refunds reach the card within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank.

## Account and team

- **Invite a team member:** **Settings > Team > Invite**, enter their email address and choose a
  role (Admin or Member). The invite link is valid for 7 days.
- **Change your email address:** **Settings > Profile > Email**. We send a confirmation link to the
  new address; the change applies once it is clicked.
- **Delete your account:** a workspace owner can delete the workspace in **Settings > Workspace >
  Delete workspace**. All data is permanently deleted after 30 days; until then the workspace can be
  restored by contacting support.
- **Reset your password:** use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for
  1 hour and can be used once. If the link says it has expired, request a new one and use only the
  newest email.

## Security and data

- All data is stored in the EU, in data centres in Frankfurt, Germany. We are GDPR compliant and
  sign a data processing agreement (DPA) on request, available in **Settings > Workspace > Legal**.
- **Two-factor authentication:** codes are sent by SMS and usually arrive within a minute. If a code
  doesn't arrive, wait 5 minutes and request a new one, or switch to an authenticator app in
  **Settings > Security**, which works without SMS.

## Integrations and API

- Pro plans include a REST API. Documentation: https://developer.helpdesk.example. Create API keys
  in **Settings > Developers**.
- Native integrations: Google Calendar, Slack and Zapier, in **Settings > Integrations**.

## Support

- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 CET, by email. There is no phone support.
- Priority support (Pro) gets a first reply within 4 business hours; other plans within 1 business
  day.
- Onboarding sessions: Pro teams with 10 or more users get one free 60-minute onboarding call.
  Book it at https://helpdesk.example/onboarding.
- Feature requests are welcome. We read every one, but can't promise if or when a feature will be
  built.

## Shipping

- We sell software only and ship no physical products.
